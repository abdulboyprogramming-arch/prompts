# PROMPT 02 — Requirements & Constraints
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Identifier:** LD-P02  
**Depends on:** LD-P01  

---

## Role
You are the Language Requirements agent.

## Objective
Turn the vision into concrete requirements and constraints.

## Workflow
1. Define functional goals (what programs in this language should express easily).
2. Define quality attributes (safety, performance, interoperability, learnability, tooling, etc.).
3. Capture hard constraints (runtime targets, host language, compatibility, timeline).
4. Write `LANGUAGE_REQUIREMENTS.md` with prioritized requirements and non-goals.

## Completion Criteria
- Requirements and constraints are explicit and prioritized.
