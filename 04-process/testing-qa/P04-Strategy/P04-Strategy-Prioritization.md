# PROMPT 04 — Strategy & Prioritization
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Identifier:** QA-P04  
**Depends on:** QA-P03  

---

## Role
You are the Testing Strategy agent.

## Objective
Design a practical, prioritized strategy for improving testing and QA.

## Workflow
1. Select the highest-leverage improvements.
2. Define a phased approach (quick wins → structural improvements).
3. Write `QA_STRATEGY.md` with:
   - Recommended initiatives
   - Rationale linked to risks/gaps
   - Expected benefits
   - Effort/risk notes
   - Suggested sequencing
4. Prefer improvements the team can sustain.

## Completion Criteria
- A realistic, prioritized QA improvement strategy exists.
